SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Integrated Court Administration

Comprehensive digital infrastructure engineered for trial efficiency, fairness, and transparency.

Case Intake & Registration

Manages case filings, party verification, and automated completeness auditing. Bail applications and personal liberty matters are automatically flagged as urgent by statutory rule.

  • ✓ Automated statutory urgency for bail petitions
  • ✓ Duplicate filing and timeline validation
  • ✓ Strict 7-stage lifecycle procedural tracking

Multi-Factor Priority Engine

Eliminates trial bottlenecks with objective mathematical scoring. Integrates legal urgency, litigant vulnerability, and waiting duration into transparent, deterministic rankings.

  • ✓ Interchangeable scoring algorithms (Weighted, FCFS, Urgency-First)
  • ✓ Itemized factor points show why each case is ranked
  • ✓ Deterministic tie-breaking ensures fair queuing

Conflict-Free Hearing Allocation

Automated calendar optimization across judges and courtrooms. Allocates earliest open slots while strictly reserving final authorization for the assigned judge.

  • ✓ 5-stage collision check prevents double-booking
  • ✓ Formal judicial approval workflow keeps judges in control
  • ✓ Live conflict auditing with manual rescheduling support
PROCEDURAL LIFECYCLE

How Cases Move Through the System

From initial filing to validated docketing and final judgment disposal.

01

Intake & Filing

Clerk registers title, parties, and classification. Statutory urgency applied automatically.

02

Completeness Audit

Validation engine inspects party names and historical dates before eligibility is granted.

03

Priority Scoring

Algorithm calculates multi-factor score based on urgency, vulnerability, and pending days.

04

Auto Scheduling

Scheduler allocates earliest conflict-free hearing slot across judicial rosters.

05

Judicial Approval

Assigned Judge authorizes the proposed hearing, reschedules, or requests modifications.
